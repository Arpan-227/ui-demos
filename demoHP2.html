A legacy of healing, a future of innovation. MediCare Plus has been the heartbeat of this community for over 28 years.

Our Story
Healing Lives, Building Trust

Founded in 1998, MediCare Plus began as a small clinic with a big dream — to make world-class healthcare accessible to all. Today, we are a 300-bed multi-specialty hospital with over 120 specialists serving 50,000+ patients annually.

We are NABH and JCI accredited, reflecting our unwavering commitment to clinical excellence, patient safety, and ethical medical practices.

🎯
Our Mission
To provide compassionate, affordable, and cutting-edge healthcare that improves the quality of life for every patient we serve.
👁️
Our Vision
To be the most trusted healthcare institution in Eastern India, setting benchmarks in clinical outcomes and patient experience.
💎
Our Values
Integrity, Compassion, Excellence, Innovation, and Teamwork guide every decision we make and every life we touch.
